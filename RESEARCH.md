# Lookout Valley Tool & Machine, Inc. — redesign research

## Live site (source of truth)

Fetched 24 Nov 2026 from https://www.lvtminc.com/ (same document is also served as https://www.lvtminc.com/index.htm).

- One page. Microsoft FrontPage 5.0, theme meta `nature 000`, table layout. No other internal pages: the only link is `mailto:keastman@lvtminc.com`. `contact.htm` and `default.htm` return 404. The theme stylesheet exists at `/_themes/nature/theme.css` but is not linked from the published page, so visitors see the inline colors, not the theme background image.
- Title, as written: Lookout Valley Tool & Machine, Inc.
- Visible copy: the legal name, `2923 Gordon Road`, `Chattanooga, TN 37419`, `(423) 825-5203`, and the footer line “Send mail to keastman@lvtminc.com with questions or comments about this web site.”
- Footer, as written: `Copyright © 2011 Lookout Valley Tool & Machine, Inc.` and `Last modified: 03/27/11`.
- Imagery: one logo, `_borders/LVTM Logo.gif` (200×200), saved as `assets/logo.gif`. A circular mark: bronze sky over an olive ridge, white letters “LVTM”, light field around the circle. No other photos.
- Line of business on the live page: not stated beyond the company name. There is no services list, no hours, no second phone, and no equipment or history copy. Directory blurbs (divisions, 1975, square footage, a fax number, weekday hours) were not copied onto this demo.

### Palette and type taken from the live page and logo

Sampled from the GIF and the HTML:

- Logo bronze sky `#9C6308` / `#945A00` (the page title is the nearby bronze `#996633`, Times New Roman).
- Logo olive ridge `#736310` / `#706010`. Logo field `#F7F7F7`.
- Body text `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`. Theme body face: Trebuchet MS, Arial, Helvetica.
- Links `#993333`, visited `#333399`, active `#006633`. Footer heading color `#336633`.

The unused theme file sets a top-bar font of `rgb(0,102,102)`, a teal. That color is not on the rendered page and is not used here. The demo stays with the bronze, olive, forest green, and maroon that the logo and the published HTML actually show.

## Google reviews

Sample, not real.

Searches for the business name plus reviews, rating, and Gordon Road did not turn up a Google rating, a Google review count, or any review text that could be quoted. The live site has no reviews. ## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, colors, or marks were copied. None of their capability lists were added, because this company’s live page does not publish one.

### Owens Industries — https://www.owensind.com/

A national precision shop. The homepage puts a short kicker over the company headline, then two actions (quote and services) before any long copy. A quote action is repeated at the bottom.

Borrowed: the hero order of place line, name, then two actions (here, Call and Email, using the published phone and mailto). A second call-to-action block near the bottom of the page. Not borrowed: their capability grid, industry tiles, certification marks, years in business, or wording.

### Peters Precision Machining — https://www.petersmachining.com/

A Joliet, Illinois machine shop. The homepage is a single headline, a trust line, one primary button, then a short row of labeled offerings, then a gallery.

Borrowed: one headline, the city kept in view, and a primary button before lower sections. Their “offerings” row is the reason the published contact facts are set as compact chips instead of large cards. Not borrowed: the ITAR / ISO line, the milling and turning labels, or the gallery (this shop published no photos beyond the logo).

### NTM, Inc. (Newport, Pennsylvania) — https://ntminc.net/

A family-owned CNC shop. The phone and email sit in the top bar. The location sits directly under the name. A quote form is high on the page, with the phone repeated beside it.

Borrowed: phone in the sticky header on wider screens, and a sticky mobile Call / Contact bar so the number is one tap away. Street address immediately under the name. A contact form is included only as a demo that submits nowhere; the working path remains the published mailto. Not borrowed: their process cards, headcount, floor size, founding year, or certification badges.
